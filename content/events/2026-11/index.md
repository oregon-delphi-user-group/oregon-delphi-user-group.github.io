Embedding resources in a compiled binary is nothing new. Delphi has stored form definitions inside every Windows VCL .EXE since day one, and the topic has come up at ODUG as far back as [June, 2000](https://odug.org/events/2000-06/), though that meeting focused on DLLs.

This month, we’ll cover storing and retrieving resources inside your own executable. We’ll demonstrate two ways to embed string data, and why you might want it as static text instead of an external file.
